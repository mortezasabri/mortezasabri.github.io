HG002 / NA24385 · four studies · one reference sample

Everything we know about calling variants
on one Genome-in-a-Bottle sample.

Four separate validation studies ran the same GIAB reference genome — HG002 — through the TSO500 v2 panel and a clinical whole-exome, and scored the results. They are gathered here in full. But they do not all ask the same question, and that is the point: three measure an assay against truth and rightly say sensitivity; one measures the panel against the exome — a fallible assay, not truth — and must say PPA. Read each in its own vocabulary.

Three of the four rows say sensitivity because they compare an assay to GIAB truth, a reference standard. The third row — panel ↔ exome — compares the panel to another assay, so by FDA convention it may only report PPA (agreement), never "sensitivity." A percentage from that study answers "do the two assays agree?", not "is the panel right?" Do not import one row's word into another.

Panel ↔ exome — agreement, not accuracy

PPA is the fraction of exome calls the panel reproduces; PPV the fraction of panel calls the exome confirms. Because the comparator is a fallible assay, these are agreement figures — FDA convention forbids the word "sensitivity" here. The low "shared-region" PPA (≈87%) is not accuracy loss: it is dominated by sites the panel is designed never to call. When the two assays disagree, GIAB arbitrates — and the two references themselves agree (Δ 0.18 pt), so the result is not an artifact of which exome callset was chosen.

vocabulary: PPA (agreement) — sensitivity is NOT allowed (non-reference comparator)

All three callsets are statistically indistinguishable against truth in the shared, attempted region. The panel's precision-for-sensitivity trade is visible only in blocklisted repeat territory it declines to call by design — where the exome's extra recoveries come with 13 false positives and the panel makes 0. Biomarkers (MSI/TMB/HRD/CNV/fusions) and LoD are not measurable on a germline sample; those panel advantages are documented from design and validation literature, not from HG002.

05

How the four fit together

Each study is a rung: two independent validations against truth, one agreement check between the assays, and one head-to-head that puts both on truth at once. Read top-to-bottom they build a single case.

rung 1 — 2

Each assay is sound on its own

The panel and the exome each pass validation against GIAB truth on their own footprints. Neither result depends on the other.

rung 3

They agree with each other

Where both assays call, the panel reproduces the exome (PPA 99.45%) — and GIAB confirms who is right at every disagreement.

rung 4

They are equivalent on shared DNA

Scored side-by-side against truth on the DNA both cover, the panel calls small variants as well as the exome. The gap is the block list, by design.

so

The MTB read-out

For shared small-variant DNA the panel is equivalent to the exome; the panel's real edge (MSI/TMB/HRD/CNV/fusions) sits outside what a germline sample can test.
