The ground they don't share

The head-to-head report scores the TSO500 panel and the whole-exome where they overlap, and finds them equivalent. But they barely overlap — the shared DNA is a rounding error of the exome and two-thirds of the panel. This page maps the territory only one assay covers, and asks what each does there — measured, as always, against GIAB truth.

02Where truth reaches · how many variants live there

Each exclusive zone is real, confident DNA — with very different variant loads

Both exclusive zones sit almost entirely inside GIAB's high-confidence regions — 97.2% of panel-only and 94.7% of exome-only — so this is ordinary, judgeable sequence, not junk. But the number of truth variants each holds could hardly be more lopsided.

The symmetry Neither assay is weak on its own turf. The panel is clean and complete on the small slice it curates (100% attempted); the exome is broad and strong across the 36 Mb it alone covers (~98–99%). They just curate for opposite goals.
04What the exclusive territory actually is

Two different kinds of "extra"

Panel-only · 0.61 Mb

  • Deeper context around the cancer genes — introns, UTRs and promoters the exome's coding-exon capture skips (97% GIAB-judgeable, so ordinary confident DNA).
  • The biomarker scaffold — the CNV backbone, MSI homopolymer loci and fusion-intron baits that make MSI / TMB / HRD / CNV / fusion calling possible.
  • Small in bp, but it is why the panel is not a small exome — this content is the price of the tumor-biomarker layer.

Exome-only · 36.08 Mb

  • The other ~19,500 genes — every coding exon outside the panel's ~520-gene cancer list.
  • Breadth for rare / novel drivers — hypothesis-free reach when the panel returns no actionable hit.
  • Germline / hereditary / secondary findings — true diploid genotypes genome-wide, which a tumor-only panel does not provide.
05Reading it together · caveats

The shared region shows equivalence; the exclusive regions show purpose

The mutual-region benchmark answers "on the DNA both cover, does the panel call small variants as well as the exome?" — yes. This page answers the next question: "how much do they even cover in common, and what is the rest for?" The answer is that the overlap is tiny, and each assay's real differentiation lives in the territory only it holds — the panel's biomarker scaffold, the exome's 20,000-gene breadth. Neither number contradicts the other; they measure different things.

Scope & caveats Autosomes 1–22 only, where GIAB v4.2.1 truth is defined (the panel's full footprint incl. X ≈ 1.97 Mb; here 1.88 Mb). Footprints are merged manifests, not per-base capture efficiency. Panel-only detection uses the same block-list "attempted" convention as the head-to-head report; exome-only detection approximates the exome's genome-wide performance. HG002 is a germline reference, so this measures small-variant detection only — the biomarker rationale in §04 is assay design, not a result from these runs.
