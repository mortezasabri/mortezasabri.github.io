About Me

I trained as a biologist and now build the bioinformatics infrastructure that turns complex omics datasets into actionable clinical and biological insight. Currently, I work as a Bioinformatician at Universitätsmedizin Greifswald (Institute of Molecular Genomics & Institute of Bioinformatics), where I build and maintain clinical cancer genomics infrastructure for the Molecular Tumor Board—including enhancement of MIRACUM-Pipe in collaboration with colleagues at University Medical Center Rostock, TSO500 somatic variant workflow automation, and methylome-based glioblastoma classification. Since 2017, I've developed and optimized pipelines for bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing, DNA methylation analysis, and genome assembly—combining hands-on wet-lab experience with advanced computational expertise. I also authored goiExplorer, an R package for interactive gene-of-interest exploration from count matrices or Salmon outputs.

Selected Projects

  • MIRACUM-Pipe — clinical maintenance & adaptation

    Originally developed by AG Boerries (University Medical Center Freiburg). I maintain and adapt it for Molecular Tumor Board (MTB) use at Universitätsmedizin Greifswald, in collaboration with colleagues at University Medical Center Rostock — QC tuning, reporting modules, and institutional workflow integration. MIRACUM-Pipe detects somatic SNVs, InDels, CNVs, and LoH from whole-exome sequencing data and generates automated clinical PDF reports, integrating functional prediction and annotation databases. Stack: R, Bash, Docker.

  • goiExplorer (R package)

    Developed this R package for interactive gene-of-interest exploration, enabling users to upload count matrix or Salmon (quant.sf) files and visualize expression patterns through built-in plotting functions.
    Features include easy installation via GitHub, comprehensive documentation, and unit tests.
    🎥 Demo: Watch on YouTube

Highlighted Pipelines

  • WES Somatic Variant Analysis (MIRACUM-Pipe)
    • Input: paired tumor/normal FASTQ files (Illumina WES)
    • Alignment: BWA-MEM; duplicate marking: Picard/GATK
    • Variant calling: SNV/InDel (Mutect2, VarScan2), CNV (CNVkit), LoH detection
    • Annotation: ANNOVAR, CIViC, OncoKB, functional prediction databases
    • Output: automated clinical PDF report for Molecular Tumor Board; collaboration with University Medical Center Rostock
  • TSO500 Somatic Panel (Illumina ICA/ICI Automation)
    • Input: FASTQ files from TSO500 targeted somatic panel (DNA + RNA)
    • Sample sheet validation and automated ICA/ICI workflow submission via CLI
    • DRAGEN TSO500 pipeline: SNV/InDel, CNV, TMB, MSI, gene fusions
    • QC filtering (coverage thresholds, contamination checks) and audit tracking
    • Output: structured variant reports for clinical MTB review
  • Methylome Classification (Glioblastoma Subgroups)
    • Input: raw IDAT files (Illumina EPIC/450k arrays) from brain tumor samples
    • Preprocessing: minfi / ChAMP (normalization, QC, batch correction)
    • Classification: reference-based methylation classifier (Heidelberg CNS classifier)
    • Output: subgroup assignment and copy number profile for clinical reporting
  • Bulk RNA-Seq (and polyA/ncRNA-Seq)
    • Input: raw FASTQ files (Illumina) from Human (AAA, Plaque samples), Mouse, and mini-Pig
    • Mapping: Bowtie2, Salmon, Kallisto, STAR, HISAT2, Subread/featureCounts, bedtools
    • DE and downstream analysis: DESeq2, edgeR, limma, GO, DO, KEGG, ReactomePA, Pubmed
  • scRNA-Seq (cell and nuclei)
    • Input: Count/feature matrix (10x Genomics) from HTTP Human blood samples and raw FASTQ files (Parse Biosciences) from Mouse brain samples.
    • Clustering, annotation, DE, and downstream analysis: Seurat and SingleCellExperiment pipelines, deconvolution analysis
  • Genome assembly
    • Input: raw FASTQ files (Illumina) from Gambusia Holbrooki
    • Mapping and scaffolding: velvet, spades, megahit, skesa, unicycler, abyss, and saute
  • Arrays
    • Input: raw IDAT files of methylation and expression datasets (Illumina) from Human and Mouse
    • DE/DM and downstream analysis: limma, GO, DO, KEGG, ReactomePA, Pubmed, etc.
  • Methyl-Seq and ChIP-Seq
    • Input: raw FASTQ files from Human and Mouse
    • Mapping: STAR
    • Peak calling and downstream analysis: ChIPseeker, DESeq2, GO, ReactomePA
